EXERCISE TIMER - INSTALL ON IPHONE AND IPAD
===========================================

Address of the app:  https://mancedavis.github.io/exercise-timer/

You do not need a computer, a terminal, Xcode, or an Apple developer account.
You only need Safari on your iPhone or iPad and an internet connection for the
first visit.

Good to know before you start
- The first visit needs the internet. After that, exercise sessions work
  without a connection.
- Your presets live on each device separately. The iPhone, the iPad and the
  Windows app do NOT sync with each other. You move presets by hand using
  small ".json" files (steps 7 to 9).
- Those exported files are your backup. If the app's data is ever removed
  (for example you delete the Home Screen app), the files are how you get
  your presets back.


STEP 1. OPEN THE APP IN SAFARI ON THE IPHONE
  1. On the iPhone, open Safari (the blue compass).
  2. Type this address in the address bar and go to it:
        https://mancedavis.github.io/exercise-timer/
  3. You should see a black screen with a big number and a Start button.
  Use Safari itself (not a link opened inside another app) so the
  "Add to Home Screen" choice appears.

STEP 2. ADD IT TO THE HOME SCREEN AND OPEN THE INSTALLED APP
  1. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up). Depending on
     your iOS version and Safari layout it is next to the address bar, at the
     bottom of the screen, or inside the "..." menu.
  2. If you see a short list, tap "View More" to see the full list.
  3. Scroll the list and tap "Add to Home Screen".
     If it is not in the list, scroll to the bottom, tap "Edit Actions",
     and add "Add to Home Screen" there.
  4. Leave "Open as Web App" switched ON, then tap "Add".
  5. Go to your Home Screen and tap the new "Exercise Timer" icon. From now on
     always start it from this icon. It opens full screen, without Safari's
     address bar.
  Note: Apple has moved these buttons around in different iOS versions, but
  the words "Add to Home Screen", "Open as Web App" and "Add" are the same.

STEP 3. REPEAT ON THE IPAD
  Do steps 1 and 2 again on the iPad. The icon only appears on the device
  where you add it, and each device keeps its own presets.

STEP 4. FINISH THE ONE-TIME ONLINE SETUP AND CHECK OFFLINE READINESS
  1. Open the app from its Home Screen icon while you are online.
  2. Look at the small label at the top right. It first says
     "Setting up offline..." and then changes to "Offline ready". That means
     everything needed is stored on the device.
  3. Wait for "Offline ready" before you rely on it without internet.
  4. To prove it: turn on Airplane Mode, close the app completely (swipe it
     away in the app switcher), and open it again from the icon. It should
     start normally.
  If it never says "Offline ready", see Troubleshooting at the end.

STEP 5. TRY A SMALL DEMONSTRATION EXERCISE
  1. Tap "Settings & presets". Set: Sets ON = 2, Reps ON = 3, Hold ON = 4,
     Rep break = 2, Get ready = 3. Tap "Done".
  2. Tap "Start". You hear a short beep when each hold starts and ends, and a
     three-note tune when the exercise is complete.
     - SOUND: if you hear nothing, turn up the volume, flip the Silent switch
       so no orange is showing (iPhone), and make sure "Sound cues" is ticked
       in Settings & presets.
  3. Tap "Pause" in the middle of a hold. The numbers freeze. Tap "Resume" and
     it carries on from exactly where it stopped.
  4. SCREEN STAYS AWAKE: while an exercise runs, the app asks the device to
     keep the screen on. Some devices or iOS versions refuse (the app shows a
     note) and some silently ignore the request. If the screen still dims or
     locks during an exercise, fix it yourself: open the iPhone or iPad
     Settings app > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock > Never. Remember to set
     it back afterwards if you like.
  5. Tap "Stop / Reset" to clear the run.

STEP 6. LEAVING THE APP OR LOCKING THE DEVICE PAUSES THE WORKOUT
  This app cannot keep timing while the screen is locked or another app is in
  front. Instead it protects your progress:
  - If you lock the device, switch to another app, or go to the Home Screen,
    the workout PAUSES immediately.
  - When you come back you will see "Paused while away". Tap "Resume" to
    continue from where you left off. Time spent away is never counted and the
    app never rushes through missed reps.
  - If iOS closes the app while you are away, the app restores your last saved
    position, paused. That saved position can be up to about 5 seconds
    behind. Tap "Resume" to continue or "Stop / Reset" to start over.

STEP 7. EXPORT PRESETS FROM WINDOWS AND GET THE FILE TO YOUR IPHONE OR IPAD
  Windows cannot AirDrop. Use one of these ordinary ways instead.
  1. In the Windows Exercise Timer, click "Export file...". Choose "Yes" for all
     presets (or "No" for just the selected one), pick a place such as the
     Desktop, and click Save. You get a file like
     exercise-timer-presets-20260115.json.
  2. Get that file onto the iPhone/iPad in either of these ways:
     - iCloud Drive: put the file in your iCloud Drive folder on Windows (iCloud
       for Windows). On the iPhone/iPad open the Files app > iCloud Drive and
       wait for it to appear.
     - Email: email the file to yourself as an attachment. On the iPhone/iPad
       open the email, tap the attachment, tap the Share button, and choose
       "Save to Files". Pick a folder you will remember (for example
       "On My iPhone").

STEP 8. IMPORT THE FILE INSIDE THE INSTALLED HOME SCREEN APP
  1. Open Exercise Timer from its HOME SCREEN ICON (not from Safari). The
     Safari tab and the Home Screen app keep separate data: presets imported
     into a Safari tab will NOT appear in the installed app.
  2. Tap "Settings & presets", then "Import file...".
  3. Choose the .json file in the Files picker.
  4. If a preset with the same name already exists, you are asked what to do:
     "Replace" (use the imported one), "Skip" (keep what you have), or
     "Cancel import" (change nothing). A tick-box lets you give the same answer
     for all the remaining conflicts.
  5. You will see a result such as "3 added, 1 replaced, 2 skipped". If
     anything is wrong with the file, you are told, and nothing is changed.
  Importing and exporting are switched off while an exercise is running or
  paused, including one the app restored after it was closed. Tap
  "Stop / Reset" first.

STEP 9. EXPORT FROM THE IPHONE OR IPAD AND BRING IT BACK TO WINDOWS
  1. In the app tap "Settings & presets", then "Export file...". If you have
     more than one preset you are asked whether to export all of them or
     only the selected one (with a single preset it just exports that one).
  2. The share sheet opens. Choose how to send the file:
     - "Save to Files" (to keep it, or to put it in iCloud Drive), or
     - AirDrop to another iPhone, iPad or Mac, or
     - Mail or Messages to yourself, or any other file-sharing app.
     If no share sheet appears, the app downloads the file instead; find it in
     the Files app under Downloads. If the file opens on the screen instead of
     saving, tap the Share button there and choose "Save to Files".
  3. If you close the share sheet without sending, nothing is wrong. The app
     simply reports "Export cancelled".
  4. On Windows, get the file onto the PC (iCloud Drive, email attachment, or
     any file transfer), then in the Windows Exercise Timer click
     "Import file..." and select it. The same Replace / Skip / Cancel choices
     apply.
  The Windows app can also import its own presets.json file
  (in %APPDATA%\ExerciseTimer) and files exported from the iPhone or iPad.

STEP 10. CONFLICTS, BACKUPS, UPDATES AND TROUBLESHOOTING
  Conflicts: names are compared ignoring capital letters ("Plank" and "plank"
  are the same preset). Nothing is changed until you have answered every
  question, and the whole import is saved in one go.

  Backups: export all presets to a .json file now and then and keep it in
  iCloud Drive or email it to yourself. Importing that file restores them.

  Updates: when a new version is available, a note "A new version is ready"
  with an "Update now" button appears. It is only usable when no exercise is
  running, and it never deletes your presets. Open the app online now and then
  to receive updates.

  Troubleshooting
  - No "Add to Home Screen" in the Share list: use Safari (not Private
    Browsing); scroll to the bottom of the share list and tap "Edit Actions" to
    add it; update iOS or iPadOS if the choice is still missing.
  - No sound: raise the volume, flip the Silent switch (no orange showing),
    tick "Sound cues", and tap Start or Resume (iOS only allows sound after a
    tap). Sound never affects the timing.
  - Import does nothing or says the file is not valid: make sure it is a .json
    file exported from Exercise Timer (or a Windows presets.json), not renamed
    or opened and re-saved in another program. Files over 256 KB are refused.
  - Imported presets are missing: you probably imported into Safari instead of
    the Home Screen app. Open the Home Screen icon and import again.
  - The label says "Offline setup incomplete (check connection)": the files
    did not finish downloading. Connect to Wi-Fi and keep the app open; the label
    changes to "Offline ready" by itself when it finishes. If it does not, close
    and reopen the app.
  - The label says "Offline not available": this browser or mode does not allow
    offline storage (for example Private Browsing in Safari). Install it from
    normal Safari as in Step 2.
  - Never says "Offline ready": open the app from the Home Screen icon while
    connected to Wi-Fi, keep it open for about 30 seconds, then close and reopen.
    If you ever remove the Home Screen icon, repeat steps 2 and 4 and re-import
    your exported file.
  - Saved presets disappeared: iOS can remove website data when storage is
    cleared or the Home Screen icon is deleted. Import your exported file.
  - Screen locks during exercise: see Step 5, point 4 (Auto-Lock > Never).

A last check only you can do: the app was tested in desktop browsers, not on a
real iPhone or iPad. On your own devices please confirm (1) Add to Home Screen
and Airplane Mode relaunch, (2) the beeps are audible, (3) the screen stays on
during a long hold, (4) locking the device pauses the workout and Resume
continues it, (5) Import picks a file from Files, and (6) Export brings up the
share sheet or saves a file you can find in Files.
